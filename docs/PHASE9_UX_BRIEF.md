# Phase 9 UX Brief

## Product purpose

The public `/share` surface is the primary résumé destination. The product should demonstrate product/frontend judgment first, data-science capability second, and quantitative/financial competence third. Its desired reaction is: "This is a thoughtful, bulletproof, creative, exceptionally well-made product."

## Audience

Design equally for the owner and first-time public viewers, but optimize the public surface for a recruiter arriving without context. Desktop is the primary presentation surface; 390px phone support must still be complete and polished.

## Personality

The product should feel experimental, curious, energetic, bold, and unconventional while remaining trustworthy. Balance: approximately 60% polished/professional and 40% playful/expressive.

## Information architecture

Use progressive disclosure like a five-course meal where visitors may stop after any course. Never use literal food language or imagery. Serve information in this order:

1. Pulse: overall performance and performance relative to the market.
2. Shape: the clean comparison chart.
3. Structure: holdings, risk, and today's movement behind explicit doors.
4. Investigation: research and company-level detail in the deep tier.
5. Laboratory: simulations and advanced comparisons in the deepest tier.

Complexity is invited, never imposed. Information is served, not dumped.

## Visual direction

Keep Phase 9's Daylight system: warm paper, Instrument Serif display moments, Inter body copy, JetBrains Mono numerals, and violet as the restrained connective accent. The memorable signature is dimensional discovery: a calm light surface that descends into the existing dark analytical tier.

Use one lightweight Portfolio Orrery as the surface's ambient three-dimensional signature. Take inspiration from spatial storytelling and floating-world composition, but do not copy the Yamauchi No.10 website, its voxel art, branding, layouts, or assets.

## Non-goals

- Do not disguise, manipulate, or visually minimize negative performance.
- Do not make a generic SaaS landing page.
- No purple-on-white gradients, glassmorphism, excessive shadows, ornamental dashboard chrome, gratuitous badges, or walls of equally weighted cards.
- No automatic audio.
- No WebGL, Three.js, React Three Fiber, new 3D dependency, or canvas-heavy environment in Phase 9.
- Do not place essential content or controls inside the decorative 3D layer.
- Do not restyle existing deep-tier components.
- Do not let animation compete with comprehension.

## UX success criteria

- A first-time visitor understands the primary fact, market context, and next action within five seconds.
- Each viewport has one dominant hierarchy and at most one primary call to action.
- Every interaction is discoverable without hover and usable by keyboard and touch.
- Financial content remains semantic, honest, readable, and functional if animation or JavaScript enhancement fails.
- Desktop feels intentionally dimensional; mobile feels intentionally simplified, not like a cropped desktop.
